source: https://aueshah.com/about/
topic: heritage_history

Aueshah traces its roots to 1987, when Syed Rashid Ali Shah founded Al-Syed Jewellers. The business was renamed Syed Murshad Jewellers in 1992 on his youngest son's fifth birthday. Following the founder's retirement in 2005, his sons rebranded as Shah's Empires and launched supporting ventures. Syed Murshad Ali Shah launched Aueshah in 2018, "blending heritage, desire, and gold to reshape modern luxury." The Noor Collection debuted in 2019 with the Noor Tiara as the company's inaugural creation.

topic: founding_story

Aueshah represents nearly four decades of jewelry craftsmanship, founded on the principle that "the rarest thing a jewel can carry is not a gemstone. It is an emotion." The brand was built not to enter the luxury world, but to redefine it entirely. In 1987, Syed Rashid Ali Shah established Al-Syed Jewellers in a city where gold was currency and trust paramount. He built slowly and deliberately, becoming known not merely for beautiful pieces but for meaningful relationships with clients — a craftsman who remembered details and understood what each creation meant to its recipient.
